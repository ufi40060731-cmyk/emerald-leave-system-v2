# Emerald Enterprise SOP Quick Start

Document status: **System SOP published / internal company policy drafts require HR approval**  
Version: 1.0  
Audience: New employees, managers, HR, and administrators

## The first 30 minutes

1. Sign in using the assigned employee account. Production mode must never fall back to shared demo accounts.
2. Select Traditional Chinese, English, or Thai.
3. Open **New employee hub** and confirm the assigned supervisor, HR contact, emergency contact, and Saturday rotation group.
4. Open **My attendance** and learn where clock-in/out records, late status, and missing punches are shown.
5. Open **SOP knowledge center**, complete the knowledge check, and acknowledge only documents marked **Published**.

## System SOP: attendance and correction

Employees may view only their own attendance records. Managers may view records for their department. HR and administrators may import and export records. A missing-punch correction must include the requested clock-in/out time and a reason. The original record and review trail must be retained.

## System SOP: Saturday rotation

Saturday work is calculated from the configured anchor Saturday, the employee's A/B rotation group, Thai holidays, and HR calendar overrides. Sunday is normally a day off unless an approved override exists. The chatbot should query the schedule database rather than guess.

## System SOP: leave request

Before submission, employees select the leave type and date range, verify the actual deductible workdays, and enter a handover note. The normal workflow is manager review followed by HR final confirmation. Returned requests must include a reason.

## Internal factory SOP placeholders

Safety/PPE, cleanroom or hygiene, quality nonconformance, confidentiality/photography, emergency response, and workplace conduct are **not official until HR, EHS, QA, or IT uploads and publishes the approved documents**. The chatbot must say that no approved answer is available rather than inventing a rule.
